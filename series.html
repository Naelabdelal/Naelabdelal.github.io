A series in seven paintings · 50 × 60 cm · 2025

Resolutions.

These seven paintings tell one story. It starts with something small lodged inside, grows into a fight with what lives there, wears the body down, and breaks open into change. At the end there is rest, and then love again.

Chapter 1 of 7

The Thorn

At first there is a thorn, slowly killing me.

A single bristling seed on a field of rust. The dark strands trailing away from it look like it is already spreading.

DanBana.

Three canvases built on the same line: a band laid straight across the picture, with growth bristling along its edges and a mark below it. From one painting to the next the band changes colour, from mauve to orange to burning red, and what lies beneath it opens up.

A collection in two series · 108 works · 2023–2024

Entropic.

The name is a play on two words: entropy and tropical. Order sliding into disorder, in hot, saturated, tropical colour. These are the earliest and smallest works, painted fast on paper and panel, where the forms of the later canvases first appear. Here they are hung the way they were made: the rows start out straight and slowly lose their hold.
