Fukface collection.

Faces keep turning up in the paint. Some were meant to be there, most simply appeared: an eye in a swirl, teeth in a crack, a mask that stares straight back. Hung together, they become a crowd, and every one of them is looking at you.

However abstract it starts, sooner or later something looks back.
