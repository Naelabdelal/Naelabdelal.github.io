A Collection of Experiments.

Acrylic paintings, 2023–2026 · 289 works, from A5 paper to 80 × 100 cm canvas.

Entropic30 × 40 cm40 × 50 cm50 × 60 cm50 × 70 cm60 × 70 cm60 × 80 cm80 × 100 cmHighlights

This collection follows a journey through scale. It began small and fast, on A5 paper and 25 × 25 cm panels in 2023, and grew step by step onto larger canvases, up to the 80 × 100 cm works of 2026: more space, more room for breathing. The parts are arranged from the smallest format to the largest, so the collection grows the way the painting did.

Elements of style · I

Flow and movement

  • Ribbons, currents and long dragged strokes
  • The eye is carried across the canvas and rarely allowed to settle
  • Even still forms seem caught in the middle of a turn
  • Direction first, shape second

Elements of style · II

Order into disorder

  • Grids, stripes and repeated marks are set down
  • then tilted, fractured and scattered
  • Entropy, from the earliest Entropic works onward
  • Order is where a painting starts, not where it ends

Elements of style · III

Living surfaces

  • Grounds like skin, stone or smoke
  • Paint dabbed, dragged and scraped until it cracks and furs
  • Black veins running through it like roots or fissures
  • Bodies, flames and glowing orbs growing out of the surface

The story of a style · 79 works, 2023–2026

A path through the collection, told in seven chapters: a few favourites from the early years, then every painting from 2025 and 2026. It follows the style as it developed, from quick colour studies on paper to the largest canvases.

Chapter I · 2023

Beginnings

It started small and fast, on A5 paper and square panels. Colour came first: spirals, waves and bursts, painted to find out what paint would do.

  • No. 17
  • No. 40
  • No. 56
  • No. 79

Chapter II · 2024

Sharpening

On the first canvases the marks grew edges. Strokes turned into needles and splinters, and lines began to end in points.

  • No. 123
  • No. 125

Chapter III · 2024

Looking back

The forms turned inward. Vortices opened onto dark centres, and eyes appeared in the middle of them.

  • No. 154
  • No. 173
  • No. 174

Chapter IV · 2024–2025

Bodies and currents

Shapes became bodies: serpents, ribbons and currents moving through the paint, and not always in peace.

  • No. 243
  • No. 247

Living surfaces

Then the ground itself came alive. Smoke-grey surfaces cracked, furred and bled, with black veins running through them.
